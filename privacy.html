Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-19

FactoryPlug ("we", "this site") is a free, read-only content site. There are no user accounts, no comments, no newsletters, and no checkout. This policy explains the limited information involved in simply visiting the site.

Information we collect

  • Almost nothing, directly. We do not ask for your name, email, or any account details, and we do not run our own analytics or tracking scripts.
  • Hosting logs. Our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) automatically records standard server logs — such as IP address, browser type, and pages visited — to operate and secure the service. We do not combine these logs with any identity.

Affiliate links and third parties

  • Product buttons link out to third-party retailers (for example, Alibaba). When you click one, that retailer's own privacy policy and cookies apply — we do not control them and cannot see your activity on their site.
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information. There is nothing to sell: we don't collect it.

Cookies

This site sets no cookies of its own. Retailers you visit through our links may set theirs; manage those in your browser settings.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about visitors, there is nothing to access, correct, or delete. If you believe otherwise, contact us via the contact page.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site evolves. The "last updated" date above will always show the current version.
