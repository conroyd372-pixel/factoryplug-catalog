Refund Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-05

The short version: FactoryPlug doesn't sell anything — there is no checkout, no cart, and no inventory. Because we never take your money, there is nothing for us to refund.

How purchases work here

  • Every product button on this site links out to a third-party supplier (for example, Alibaba). When you buy, your order — and your money — goes directly to that seller.
  • Your purchase contract is with the seller, not with us. Refunds, returns, and exchanges are governed by the seller's own policy, which you should read on their listing before paying.
  • We may earn an affiliate commission when you buy through our links, at no extra cost to you. That commission doesn't change your refund rights with the seller.

If something goes wrong with an order

  • Contact the seller first. Most suppliers resolve wrong-item, damaged, or missing shipments directly — message them through the platform you ordered on.
  • Use the platform's buyer protection. On Alibaba, orders placed with Trade Assurance include dispute and refund mechanisms. File through the order page if the seller won't resolve it.
  • Document everything. Photos of the received item, packaging, and your order confirmation make disputes far easier to win.
  • Confirm fitment before you buy. Most "wrong part" disputes come down to fitment — always confirm your exact year, model, trim, engine, and VIN with the seller before money moves. See our fitment disclaimer.

Questions

We can't issue refunds (we never charged you), but if you need help figuring out a dispute with a seller, reach us at support@thefactoryplug.com.
