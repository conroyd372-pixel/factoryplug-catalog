What Alibaba Trade Assurance Actually Covers

By Free sourcing by RoyblingE90 — free parts sourcing, no gatekeeping.

The short answer

Alibaba Trade Assurance is escrow with rules: Alibaba holds your payment and releases it only when the agreed order terms are met. It covers non-shipment, late dispatch, and products that arrive materially different from the invoice specs. It does not cover wrong-fitment guesses, buyer's remorse, or promises outside the written order terms.

What it is

Trade Assurance is Alibaba's order-protection program. When your order is covered, Alibaba holds your payment and only releases it to the seller when the order terms are met. Think of it as escrow with rules.

What it covers

  • Payment protection — if the seller never ships, you get your money back.
  • On-time dispatch — if the seller misses the agreed ship date, you're covered.
  • Product as described — if the part arrives materially different from the agreed specs, you can claim.

What it does NOT cover

  • Fitment guesses. If you ordered the wrong size for your car and the part matches the listing, that's on the buyer. Confirm year, model, trim, and VIN with the seller first.
  • Buyer's remorse. Changed your mind? That's not a claim.
  • Expectations beyond the contract. Only what's written in the order terms counts — verbal promises in chat help, but the invoice rules.

How to file

Contact the seller first and give them a chance to fix it — most disputes settle here. If that fails, open a claim from your order page with photos, the invoice, and your chat history. Document damage with unboxing video; claims live or die on evidence.

Quick answers

What does Alibaba Trade Assurance cover?

Payment protection if the seller never ships, on-time dispatch per the agreed date, and products that arrive materially different from the agreed specs. Alibaba holds your payment until the terms are met.

Does Trade Assurance cover wrong fitment?

No. If the part matches the listing but doesn't fit your car because you ordered the wrong size, that's on the buyer. Confirm year, model, trim, and VIN with the seller first.

How do I file a Trade Assurance claim?

Contact the seller first, then open a claim from your order page with photos, unboxing video, the invoice, and your chat history. Claims are won on evidence.
