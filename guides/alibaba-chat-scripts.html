Alibaba Chat Scripts: Exactly What to Send the Factory

By Free sourcing by RoyblingE90 — free parts sourcing, no gatekeeping.

The short answer

Professional Alibaba buyers confirm fitment in writing before paying: send your exact chassis code, your full 17-digit VIN, and the OE number off your existing part, plus photos of mounting points and connectors. For custom wheels, ask for final CAD drawings confirming bolt pattern, center bore, and offset tolerances. For dry carbon, get the factory to confirm pre-preg autoclave curing in writing.

Talk to the factory like a professional buyer

In global B2B automotive sourcing, the buyer who gets the right part the first time isn't the one who haggles hardest — it's the one who specs first. Factories work from data, not descriptions. Below are three copy-paste message templates that give the factory engineer everything needed to confirm your order. Use them before you pay; the factory's written reply becomes part of your order record.

1. The Fitment Guarantee Request

Send this for any bolt-on part — exhausts, downpipes, body kits, brakes, intakes, suspension. Fill in the bracketed fields with your real data: your exact chassis code (F30, G80, S650, and so on), your full 17-digit VIN (driver-side dash, door jamb, or B-pillar sticker), and the OE number stamped on your existing component.

Hello. I am sourcing a [Part Name] for a [Year] [Make] [Model]. My chassis code is [Code] and my 17-digit VIN is [VIN]. Can you confirm in writing that your component is a direct fit for OE Number [Number] without any modifications?

2. The Wheel Customization Request

Send this before paying for custom forged wheels. Real wheel factories build to CAD drawings — asking for the final drawings with your bolt pattern, center bore, and offset tolerances is standard engineering practice, and it locks the spec before metal gets cut.

Before processing payment for these custom forged monoblock wheels, please provide the final CAD drawings confirming the [Bolt Pattern], [Center Bore], and [Offset] tolerances for my specific chassis.

3. The Material Specification Request

Send this when ordering dry carbon fiber parts. True dry carbon is pre-preg material cured in an autoclave; lower-cost parts are wet-lay fiberglass with a carbon overlay. This one sentence gets the factory to state the process in writing — which is exactly what you want on the record.

I am ordering the dry carbon fiber [Part Name]. Please confirm this is pre-preg carbon fiber cured in an autoclave, not wet-lay fiberglass with a carbon overlay.

Three professional habits

  • Keep it in the Alibaba chat. Every message and reply is timestamped in your order record — that's your evidence if the wrong part ships. See what to do when an order arrives damaged.
  • Send photos with the message. Clear shots of your mounting points and connectors let the engineer catch a mismatch before production or packing.
  • Get the confirmation in writing before payment. A one-line “yes, confirmed it fits” in the chat log is standard procedure — professional buyers never skip it.

Quick answers

What should I send the factory before ordering a car part on Alibaba?

Your exact chassis code and trim, your full 17-digit VIN, the OE number off your existing part, and clear photos of the mounting points and connectors. Factories work from data, not descriptions — this is standard procedure in B2B automotive sourcing.

How do I confirm custom wheels will fit before paying?

Ask the factory for the final CAD drawings confirming bolt pattern, center bore, and offset tolerances for your specific chassis. Get that confirmation in writing before money moves.

How do I verify a carbon fiber part is really dry carbon?

Ask the factory to confirm in writing that it is pre-preg carbon fiber cured in an autoclave, not wet-lay fiberglass with a carbon overlay. The written answer becomes part of your order record.

Why keep the conversation in the Alibaba chat?

Every message is timestamped in your order record — that is your evidence if the wrong part ships. Never move payment or key promises off-platform.
