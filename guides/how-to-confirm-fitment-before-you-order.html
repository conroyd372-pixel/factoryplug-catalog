How to Confirm Fitment Before You Order

By Free sourcing by RoyblingE90 — free parts sourcing, no gatekeeping.

The short answer

Never guess fitment. Before paying, send the seller your exact year, model and chassis code, trim and engine, VIN, and relevant measurements (bolt pattern, offset, and center bore for wheels). A good seller confirms “yes, this fits VIN ___” in writing. “Fits all models” or an instant yes with no questions is a red flag.

The rule

No listing page — ours or anyone's — can guarantee a part fits your exact car. Fitment is confirmed with the seller, before payment, using your car's real details. Everything else is a guess, and guesses get expensive.

The checklist: send all five

  • Year — model years change mounting points mid-generation.
  • Model and chassis code — "3 Series" isn't enough; E90 vs F30 vs G20 are different cars underneath.
  • Trim and engine — brakes, intakes, and exhausts vary by engine and package.
  • VIN — the seller can decode exact factory specs from it. This is the single best thing you can send.
  • Photos/measurements — for wheels: bolt pattern, center bore, offset range. For aero: a photo of the mounting area.

What a good seller does

They confirm in writing: "Yes, this fits VIN ___." They may ask for measurements or photos back. That back-and-forth is a good sign — it means they're actually checking, not just saying yes to get the sale.

Red flags

"Fits all models," instant "yes" with no questions asked, or refusing to confirm against your VIN. If they won't put fitment in writing, don't put money down.

Quick answers

How do I confirm a part fits my car before ordering on Alibaba?

Send the seller your year, model and chassis code, trim, engine, VIN, and relevant measurements. Get “yes, this fits VIN ___” confirmed in writing before you pay.

Is my VIN safe to share with an Alibaba seller?

Yes — sharing your VIN lets the seller decode exact factory specs and confirm fitment. It's the single best thing you can send, and standard practice in parts sourcing.

What wheel measurements do I need for fitment?

Bolt pattern (e.g. 5x114.3), center bore, diameter, width, and offset range — plus your year, model, and whether the car is lowered.
