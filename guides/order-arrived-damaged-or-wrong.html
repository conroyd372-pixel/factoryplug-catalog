What to Do When Your Order Arrives Damaged or Wrong

By Free sourcing by RoyblingE90 — free parts sourcing, no gatekeeping.

The short answer

If your Alibaba order arrives damaged or wrong: film the unboxing immediately, photograph everything against the invoice, message the seller the same day with a clear ask (replacement, partial refund, or return), and keep all messages inside Alibaba's chat. If they won't fix it, file a Trade Assurance claim with your photos, video, and chat history.

Step 1 — Document immediately

Film the unboxing before you touch anything: sealed box, shipping labels, damage as you find it. Photograph the part next to the packing slip and the invoice specs. Timestamp everything. Claims are won on evidence, and evidence rots fast.

Step 2 — Contact the seller first

Message them the same day with photos and a clear ask: replacement, partial refund, or return. Most established sellers will negotiate — a replacement shipment or a 20–40% refund for cosmetic damage is a common settlement. Keep every message inside Alibaba's chat.

Step 3 — File a Trade Assurance claim if they won't fix it

Open the claim from your order page. Attach the unboxing video, photos, invoice, and chat history showing the seller's refusal or silence. Be specific about the remedy you want and why the order terms support it.

Step 4 — Know the realistic outcomes

  • Partial refund — most common for cosmetic damage or minor spec misses. Take it if the part is usable.
  • Replacement — best for wrong or non-functional parts from a seller you still trust.
  • Full refund — usually requires returning the part, and return shipping to China can eat half the refund. Do the math first.

The last resort

If Trade Assurance fails and you paid by credit card through Alibaba, a chargeback is the final lever — but it burns the bridge with the platform and the seller. Use it only when every other path is genuinely dead.

Quick answers

What should I do if my Alibaba order arrives damaged?

Film the unboxing immediately, photograph everything against the invoice specs, and message the seller the same day with a clear ask — replacement, partial refund, or return. Keep all messages inside Alibaba's chat.

Can I get a refund for a wrong item on Alibaba?

Yes. Contact the seller first — most will negotiate a replacement or partial refund. If they won't fix it, file a Trade Assurance claim with your photos, video, and chat history.

Should I accept a partial refund for a damaged part?

Often yes — partial refunds of 20–40% are the most common settlement for cosmetic damage, and full refunds usually require return shipping to China, which can eat half the refund.
