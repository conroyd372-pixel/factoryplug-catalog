🛞 Tire Size Calculator

Compare any two tire sizes in seconds. Enter your current size and the new size you're considering — we'll show the diameter difference, speedometer error, and a straight verdict.

Tire size 225/45R17 reads as 225 millimeters of tread width, a 45% sidewall aspect ratio, radial construction, and a 17-inch rim. Overall diameter is (width × aspect ratio ÷ 100 × 2 ÷ 25.4) + rim diameter — about 25 inches here. Stay within 3% of your stock diameter and your speedometer, gearing, and clearances all behave.

How to read the result

Within 3%
Safe zone — speedometer stays accurate, clearances behave like stock.
3–5%
Check clearances carefully and expect a small speedometer error.
Over 5%
Too far — gearing, speedometer, and ABS behavior all shift, and rubbing risk climbs.

The math behind it

Overall diameter = (width × aspect ratio ÷ 100 × 2 ÷ 25.4) + rim diameter. The percentage difference between old and new is what your speedometer feels: a new tire 3% taller means your speedo reads about 3% slow at any speed.
