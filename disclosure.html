The short version: some links on this site earn us a commission if you buy — it costs you nothing extra, and it's what keeps the lights on and the catalog free.

Affiliate disclosure

  • FactoryPlug participates in affiliate programs, including the Alibaba affiliate program.
  • When you click a product link and make a purchase, we may earn a commission at no additional cost to you.
  • Commissions never influence which products earn a spot in the catalog — every listing must first clear our quality bar (strong supplier rating, real transaction history, sensible price, low MOQ).
  • Prices shown are ranges from the supplier's listing and can change; the live price on the retailer's page is what counts.

Fitment disclaimer — read this

  • We never guarantee fitment. A listing page cannot confirm a part fits your exact car.
  • Always confirm your year, model, trim, engine, and VIN directly with the factory contact before money moves.
  • Photos are for reference — the exact finish, hardware, and included accessories are defined by the seller's listing.
  • Carbon-ceramic brake and big-brake-kit buyers: we route these to our trusted ICOOH factory line — still confirm application directly with the factory contact.

Why factory-direct?

Many big brands white-label factory parts and mark them up several times over. Buying closer to the manufacturer cuts that markup out. Our job is checking the supplier first, so you don't learn the hard way.
